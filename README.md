# 📸 Viral AI Avatar Creator Workflow (Featuring MeraCollege)

Welcome to this repository! Here, I have documented a simple and viral AI image generation workflow. Using a specific website and tools like **ChatGPT, Gemini, or Bing Image Creator**, you can create stunning, personalized AI photos (like trending 3D social media avatars) with your own face!

## 🚀 What is this workflow?

There is a website called [meracollege.net](https://meracollege.net/) (Don't let the name confuse you, it's a treasure trove for AI prompts!). It provides ready-to-use, highly detailed prompts for viral AI images. 

By copying these prompts and using them with advanced AI bots, you can recreate the exact same viral images, but customized with **your name** and **your face**.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these steps to get your own personalized AI photo:

### Step 1: Get the Prompt
1. Visit [meracollege.net](https://meracollege.net/).
2. Browse through the trending AI photo templates (e.g., 3D boy/girl sitting on a social media logo, glowing name on a hoodie, etc.).
3. Select your favorite style and **copy the prompt** provided on the page.

### Step 2: Generate the Base Image
1. Open your favorite AI Image Generator:
   - **ChatGPT** (with DALL-E 3)
   - **Google Gemini** 
   - **Bing Image Creator**
2. **Paste the prompt** into the chat.
3. *Important:* Edit the prompt to replace the default name with **your own name** (e.g., Change "Rahul" to "YourName").
4. Hit generate! You will get a high-quality 3D/AI image matching the website's style.

### Step 3: Put Your Own Face on It (The Magic Step ✨)
To make the AI character look exactly like you, you have two options depending on the tool you are using:
- **Option A (If using ChatGPT/Gemini):** Upload a clear, front-facing photo of yourself along with the prompt and ask the AI: *"Use this photo as a face reference and generate the image using the prompt above."*
- **Option B (Face Swap Tools):** Take the AI-generated image (from Step 2) and your real photo. Go to any free AI Face Swap tool (like Remaker AI or similar) and swap your face onto the AI character. 

🎉 **Boom! You now have a viral, studio-quality AI image with your own face.**
